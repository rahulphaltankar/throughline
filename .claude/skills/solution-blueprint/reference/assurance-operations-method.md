# Assurance, Deployment and Operations Method

Map important acceptance criteria to tests/evidence. Distinguish deterministic tests, technical validation, UAT, AI evaluation, operational readiness and real-world/business validation.

Deployment covers environments, release, migration, cutover, rollback and production-readiness conditions.

Operations covers ownership, support tiers, SLA/SLO, monitoring, logs/metrics/traces, alerting, incident/problem/change, runbooks, resilience, backup/recovery and AI/model monitoring where relevant.
