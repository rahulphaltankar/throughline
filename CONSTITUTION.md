# Throughline Constitution

Version: 0.1

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Throughline may recommend; it may not silently assume authority.
2. The kernel must remain deterministic and contain no LLM calls.
3. Production access is deny-by-default and outside the blueprint MVP.
4. Claims must carry provenance and an evidence class.
5. Acceptance criteria must be frozen before major downstream solutioning.
6. The same invocation that authors an artefact must not be its sole evaluator.
7. Regulatory/legal classifications are advisory unless accepted by an accountable human.
8. No irreversible action is implied by a plan.
9. Unknown information must be represented explicitly.
10. Material changes invalidate downstream artefacts until re-evaluated.

## Human accountability

Human approval is mandatory for: organisational priority, legal/regulatory attestation, residual-risk acceptance, production-data access, contracts/money, regulated decisions, first production release, and decommissioning.
